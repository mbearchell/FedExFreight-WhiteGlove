Get set up — 5 minutes

Before we start

1 · Get the demo data and save it to OneDrive

Open the demo data library →

  1. Open the demo data library with the button above.
  2. Click the “Download” button next to each of the 8 files.
  3. The 8 files will land in your Downloads folder.
  4. In OneDrive, create a new folder called “Copilot Demo” and copy the 8 files into it.
  5. Open one file from the Copilot Demo folder to confirm it’s there. Copilot can only reference files that live in OneDrive or SharePoint.
Excel tip: open the Operations & Finance workbook and make sure AutoSave (top-left) is ON — Copilot needs the file in OneDrive to analyze it.

2 · Readiness checklist

Tick these off before the first prompt. If anything’s missing, flag it now.

No Copilot icon in an app? You may need the license assigned or a quick restart of the app — raise your hand and we’ll sort it before we begin.
Module 1 · Catch up fast

Microsoft 365 Copilot App

File: Your own email & Teams (live, read-only)  ·  Fallback: CFG_Email_and_Teams_Threads.docx

Open the Microsoft 365 Copilot app (or the Copilot app in Teams) in Work mode. This reads your real inbox and Teams and hands you a triage — and it only ever sees what you already have access to.

Summarize my unread email from the last 7 days and list anything that needs a reply or a decision from me.

What did I miss in my Teams chats and channels this week? List any action items assigned to me and who is waiting on me.

Catch me up on everything related to the FedEx Freight launch across my email and chats, and tell me what still needs my attention.

Fallback

Summarize CFG_Email_and_Teams_Threads.docx and list the open action items with their owners.

Helpful: reference a file by typing / then its name. This is all read-only — nothing is sent, moved, or changed. Notice Copilot only surfaces what you can already see; that’s your built-in security guardrail.
Module 2 · Make sense of a contract

Word

File: CFG_Transition_Services_Agreement.docx  ·  Stretch: CFG_Carrier_Service_Agreement.pdf

Open the file in Word, then click the Copilot icon on the Home ribbon and paste a prompt.

Summarize this agreement in plain language: what services Meridian Global Logistics is providing to Contoso Freight Group, for how long, and what we are obligated to pay or deliver in return.

Create a table of every key date, milestone, fee, and termination or exit condition in this agreement.

List the top five risks or ambiguous terms a VP should raise with Legal. For each one, quote the section it comes from.

Draft a half-page summary memo of this agreement for my operations leaders who will not read the full contract.

Helpful: Copilot quotes the exact section it pulled from — handy when you hand findings to Legal. The same prompts work on the PDF carrier agreement through Copilot Chat.
Module 3 · Find the story in the numbers

Excel

File: CFG_Operations_and_Finance_Data.xlsx

Open in Excel. First, click inside the data and press Ctrl+T to format it as a Table, and make sure AutoSave is ON — Copilot’s analysis needs both.

Analyze this data and give me the three most important trends in our service performance and cost this month.

Add a column that calculates cost per shipment and flag the bottom five performers.

Create a PivotTable that summarizes total shipments, tonnage, and revenue by service center.

Create a service center performance dashboard on a new worksheet. Include a summary by service center with shipments, tonnage, revenue, on-time delivery percentage, and cost per shipment, add charts for on-time performance and cost per shipment, and highlight the top and bottom five performers.

Helpful: if a column name in a prompt doesn’t match your sheet, just swap in the real header. Stuck? Ask: What questions can you answer about this data?
Module 4 · Build the executive update

PowerPoint

File: CFG_Month1_Business_Review_Brief.docx → a new deck  ·  Fallback: CFG_Starter_Deck.pptx

Open a blank presentation, click Copilot, and choose Create presentation from file.

Create a presentation from CFG_Month1_Business_Review_Brief.docx for a leadership audience. Keep it to about eight slides.

Add an executive scorecard slide summarizing our Month 1 operational KPIs.

Rewrite the speaker notes on the opening slide so I can deliver it in under 60 seconds.

Helpful: this is a strong first draft to edit, not a finished deck. If “Create from file” is slow, open CFG_Starter_Deck.pptx and ask Copilot to summarize it in three takeaways and apply a cleaner, more executive design.
Module 5 · Communicate it

Outlook

File: A new email + a real thread in your inbox

In a new email, choose Draft with Copilot. Then open a long thread and use Copilot to Summarize. Nothing sends until you press Send.

Draft an email to my service-center directors sharing our Month 1 results and the top three priorities for next month. Tone: confident, concise, no jargon.

Make it shorter, add a clear call to action, and give me a one-line subject.

Summarize this thread and list any action items assigned to me.

Helpful: try Coaching by Copilot on a draft — it scores tone and clarity and suggests improvements before you send.
Wrap-up

Make it yours

Pick the prompt that matches your world, run it, and let’s hear a few results.

Pick your function

Operations

List the operational risks in the Month 1 brief and group them by likelihood and impact.

Finance / Accounting / FP&A

From the operations and finance data, summarize the main cost drivers and where margin is leaking by service center.

Data & Analytics

What additional data fields would make this operations dataset more useful for trend analysis?

Commercial / Sales

Draft a customer-facing summary of our service performance improvements this month.

Safety & Recovery

Scan the Month 1 brief for any safety or recovery-operations items and turn them into a watch list.

Real Estate / Facilities

Based on the service-center data, which locations show the most strain and may need a capacity review?

IT / Engineering / Enterprise Apps

Summarize the transition services agreement and flag every IT system, application, or service we depend on Meridian Global Logistics for.

Enterprise Productivity / Adoption

Draft a five-step tip sheet to help my team start using Copilot this week.

Bonus prompts, if you have time

Compare CFG_Transition_Services_Agreement.docx and CFG_Carrier_Service_Agreement.pdf and tell me where our obligations overlap or conflict.
